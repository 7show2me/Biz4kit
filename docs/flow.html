How a sale moves through Biz4Kit

A customer either asks for a price or books a slot. Both are Leads. The business replies with a price, and that reply is the invoice. Paying it is the acceptance. Biz4Kit is a brand kit, not a CRM: the core is track the lead and four Quick jobs: Catalog · Reply · Invoice · Cashflow (money in only, D77).20, 23

1. What is what

One word per concept, used the same way in the UI, messages, PDFs and code.3 Status colours never repeat within one record.19 The crossed-out words are the ones we do not use.

Lead

Anything a customer requested. Two types: Quoted asked for a price, Booked booked a slot.20

NewContactedRepliedSnoozedRejectedPaid

Made by: the customer (Request a quote, or Book) or Staff by hand.1

Not: enquiry (allowed in customer copy), prospect, opportunity, quote (there is no Quote record, D60)

Invoice

A numbered request for payment, linked to the lead. The reply to a quoted lead is a proforma PI-: not a tax invoice. When the payment is approved it becomes the tax invoice INV- with the next gap-free number. Edits before payment are versions of the same PI-. Every invoice, including one after the service, stays PI- until its payment is approved (D71). An advance and its balance are two invoices, each PI- then INV- (D70). An unpaid reply is not money owed: past its pay-by date it turns Expired, never Overdue (D75).3, 6, 20, 23

Bill: DraftDuePart paidOverduePaidWaiting for approval (Part paid: some approved, some still due; Overdue wins after the due date, D55)
Reply: Awaiting paymentWaiting for approvalPaidExpired (a reply has no Part paid: an advance and its balance are two invoices, D70)

Made by: Staff, when they Accept a quoted lead, after a booked lead's service, or from manual lines.16 A shared invoice is cancelled (void) only by the Owner and keeps its number; a draft can be deleted by anyone who writes.5

Not: bill, receipt, estimate, quotation

Serial numbers and warranty (an extra, D85, D86): model and warranty or guarantee on the proforma; the serial on the tax invoice; one row per unit once serials are entered; a warranty card per row with a serial.

Optional lines: the customer ticks them on the pay page and the amount updates as they tick (D102).

Booking

A reserved time with a staff member, from a booked lead or set after a quoted lead is paid.3

To confirmAgreedIn progressCompletedCancelledNo-show (Cancelled red, D100)

Confirmed by: the business, with a staff member assigned (D62). A clash on member + time is refused. Slots fall inside the business's opening hours: up to two ranges per weekday, starting every 30 minutes and fitting the package length; a day with no range is closed (D97, D99).4

Not: slot, reservation; “appointment” allowed for salons

Job extra

The work done for a paid reply or an Agreed booking (D69), with a checklist, before / after photos and materials used (D87). Marking it Done makes the draft invoice at once (a bill, or the balance after an advance); the next step is Review and send (D88). An extra the owner switches on (D63).3

OpenIn progressDoneInvoiced

Not: service, task, order

Payment and proof

A payment request is the UPI link, QR and bank details on the invoice page. A proof is the screenshot or PDF uploaded. Approve is an approver confirming it: the only path to Paid in Phase 1.3, 4

Waiting for approvalApprovedRejected

Uploaded by: the customer on the pay page, or the Owner, a Manager or Staff; the Accountant is read-only plus export (D91). Approved by: Owner, or a Manager strictly below the limit the Owner sets (D57, D74). When the Owner is the only approver they may approve their own upload, logged as self-approved (D74). Approval above the open balance is refused (D76).

Not: screenshot, verify, payment link (gateway, Phase 2)

Customer CRM is extra

The person or company the business sells to. Never has an account; acts only through private links. One per phone number. The customer list and timeline are extras (D63).3, 4

Sees: the invoice page and a private My orders page listing every request, booking, invoice and receipt; a warranty card for each item with a serial (D86).2 “Made with Biz4Kit” shows only on the catalog, request, booking and My orders pages, never on the pay page, PDF or warranty card (D89).

Not: user, buyer; “client” allowed for agencies and IT

2. Two doors in

The customer lands on the business’s own page from a QR code, a WhatsApp link or an Instagram bio.1 Looking at the catalog or the price list creates nothing. Two things create a lead:

Request a quote → Quoted lead

Basket of items or a free-text brief, photos, preferred date, location, name and phone.1 The business prices it and replies with a proforma; the customer pays to confirm.

When the price depends on the job: bridal, catering, a wholesale order, a website, a batch of parts. Also when the owner hides a price (“ask for a quote”).

Book a package → Booked lead

Only for packages the owner marks bookable. The customer picks a slot inside the business's opening hours (D97, D99); the business assigns a staff member and confirms.1 The price is the package price.

When the price is fixed and it is a time slot: a hair spa, a monthly package, a trial session.

There is no checkout or cart. “Our customers ask or book; they don’t check out.”13

4. Overdue payments D64

A bill owed after the service (post-service or balance invoice) turns Overdue by itself the day after its pay-by date.5 An unpaid reply PI- is not money owed and never goes overdue: it turns Expired and gets a follow-up (message 6) (D75). The Overdue page under Payments (D80) lists every one with the customer, days overdue, amount and the last reminder, and a one-tap Remind by Email or SMS.

When Ready message Who sends it
+3 days 13, gentle: “a gentle reminder that invoice {invoice_no} ({amount}) from {business} is due. Pay here: {link}”11 Email: the app sends it. SMS and WhatsApp: copy and paste (D67). Phase 2 sends all three on schedule
+7 and +14 days 14, overdue: “invoice {invoice_no} for {amount} is now {days} days overdue. Please pay at {link} or reply if there is an issue. — {business}”11 Same

The Owner, Manager and Accountant see the page; only Owner and Manager get the Remind button. The Overdue card on Today opens it. Every Remind writes a Message row on the invoice, so the timeline shows what was sent.11

5. When approval is needed

Four gates. They are the same for every business type; only the Manager amount limit is set per workspace.7, 10

Gate Who What is required What it unlocks
Accept, Edit or Reject a request The business (Staff, Manager, Owner) Accept: price every line, pay-by date, advance % if any (the reply is then the advance proforma, each line at that %, D70); lines may be marked optional for the customer to tick (D102). Reject: a reason and the decline message (18, wording D95). D61. Accept creates and shares the proforma; Lead → Replied. Reject → Rejected.
Confirm a booking The business A staff member assigned; To confirm → Agreed (D69). Overlapping bookings for one member are refused.5 D62. The customer gets message 10; the service can start.

There is no customer-side Accept button any more: paying the proforma is the acceptance (D60). Staff cannot see reports or the approval queue; the Accountant is read-only with export: cannot record, approve or edit (D91).9

6. How it changes by business type

The flow and the gates are identical. A starter kit changes the labels, the default units, whether HSN and MOQ fields show, and what follows a paid reply (D56, read with “quote” as “quoted lead” and “accept” as “pays”, D66; tax invoice on approval for every type, D90). Starter kits for the five types after salon are drafted by Claude and approved by the owner (D96) Open.10 Pilot verticals are salons and design / IT agencies.22

8. Phase 1 and Phase 2

Phase 1 (this build)

  • Three channels: Email, SMS, WhatsApp. The app sends email; SMS and WhatsApp are ready to copy and paste. Every one is logged (D67).11
  • Automatic anyway: Replied on share, Overdue past the pay-by date (bills only; an unpaid reply turns Expired, D75), tax invoice and Paid when approvals reach the total, draft invoice (bill or balance) on job done (D88).5
  • Email to the assignee for a new lead, to the approver when a proof waits (with the approve-from-email link), copies to the customer when an email is known.12 In-app notification inbox behind the bell, by role, with unread and per-person mute (D87).
  • 16 ready messages: 8 and 9 (“Quote accepted”) are retired; 4–7 and 18 wording approved (D93, D95).
  • Paid means the owner approved a proof, not that a bank confirmed it.17

Phase 2 later

  • Messages sent automatically: reminders +3 / +7 / +14, booking reminders; WhatsApp API.
  • Gateway links (Razorpay, Stripe, Polar) with webhook reconcile; unmatched payments drop into the approval queue.7
  • OTP on My orders where the owner wants it.2
  • Recurring invoices, credit notes, instant receipts.16

9. Where the docs disagree Docs disagree

  • Quote record. prep/ still describes a Quote entity, a quote editor, a customer Accept button and lawyer-reviewed accept wording. D60 and D61 replace all of that; inline notes are in Design inputs.md (glossary, data model, state table, Flow 2). Legal & compliance.md 108–114 (accept wording) is now unused.20
  • Invoice statuses. Outline says Draft → Sent → Paid → Overdue; Design inputs has Part paid and Void. This page uses Design inputs: Part paid (teal, D55) for a bill with some approved and some due; a reply has none, because an advance and its balance are two invoices (D70).
  • Board labels. Outline lines 26 and 54 differ; both are replaced by D65.
  • Manager approval. Two wordings in Design inputs; settled as one setting, D57.
  • Numbering example. INV-2026-001 in Outline versus the financial-year form; the FY form is the rule, and PI- is a separate series (D60).6
