Every status in Biz4Kit

Seven record types. Each one moves left to right. Under each status: what moves it there. One colour never repeats inside a row (D55).

What each colour means

Blue
New. Something just arrived or is running now.
Grey
Not yet. Nothing has happened, or it is parked.
Violet
Waiting on us. The business must act or approve.
Amber
Waiting on the customer. A date or a payment is open.
Teal
Half done or firm. Part delivered, or a slot Agreed (D68).
Green
Done. Money in or work finished (D68).
Red
Problem. Late, or the customer said no.

Booking words: decided (D69)

Only one pair is needed. The rule from the colour key: the firm slot is "done" (green or teal), the held slot is "not yet" (grey) or "waiting on the customer" (amber).

Firm slot Held slot Reads as Fits the rest
Confirmed Tentative Before. "Tentative" is a long word on a phone. Green repeats Paid in the board row; amber reads as a problem.
Agreed To confirm Plain Indian English. "Agreed" says both sides said yes to the slot. Teal matches the Booked board column. Grey says "not yet", not "trouble".
Booked Not confirmed Clear, but "Booked" is already the lead type chip. Two "Booked" labels next to each other on a lead.
Confirmed Hold Short. "Hold" is hotel language. Keeps green; only the held word changes.
Fixed Waiting for customer Says who we wait for. Long; amber means the same as Due on an invoice, which is right.
