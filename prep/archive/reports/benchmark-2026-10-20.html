Where the flow breaks

Lead→ Reply = PI-→ Advance / balance?→ Proof upload→ Approval (limit, 1-person)→ INV- number→ Lead "Paid"?

Red steps contradict each other across docs or are undecided. The decisions agree on the model (lead → PI- → approval → INV-), but the prototype doesn't follow it.

Questions for you (these decide the flow; I won't guess them)

1 · Advance and balance

A: one PI, part paid, INV- at full payment · B: advance PI → its own INV-, then a balance PI → INV- · C: advance receipt, one INV- at service

Recommended: B (matches flow.html). CA to confirm GST.

2 · Post-service invoice before payment

A: PI- until approved, like replies · B: INV- at first share

Recommended: A (one rule). CA to confirm.

3 · When is a lead "Paid"?

A: first payment approved · B: every invoice on it fully paid · C: reply PI paid only

Recommended: B

4 · Payment after Reject / Snooze / expiry

A: block, the pay page says "no longer valid" · B: accept, approval shows a warning

Recommended: B for Snoozed, A for Rejected or expired

5 · Manager limit, one-person shop

Limit "below" (<) or "up to" (≤)? When the owner is the only approver, may they approve their own upload (logged)?

Recommended: <, and yes (logged)

6 · Is an unpaid proforma money owed?

A: yes, it can go Overdue with reminders 13/14 · B: no, it becomes "Expired" after pay-by and gets a follow-up

Recommended: B

7 · Overpay / paid against an old PI version

Refuse approval above the balance, or approve and refund outside the app? Apply to latest version or hold for owner?

Recommended: refuse above balance; apply to latest, note it

8 · Scope: Quick Cashflow & Quick Catalog

D63 names leads + Quick Invoice + Quick Reply. Are Catalog and Cashflow now core too? Does Cashflow include money out (expenses / cashbook) in Phase 1?

Needs your call: money out is a scope change

Suggested order

  1. Now, no decisions needed: fix sample data (GST, Due totals, Kavya, Asha, reminders), manager limit button, single primary button on the reply sheet, wire Share price list.
  2. After questions 1–3 and 6: numbering (PI-/INV-), advance/balance states, board mapping, lead Paid.
  3. Then the missing Quick screens: New invoice composer, ready-message picker, "To collect this week" card.
