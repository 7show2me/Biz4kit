Sidebar: new names

Only the five section names change. The pages inside each section are tabs (owner, 21 Oct) and keep the glossary names from docs/flow.html: Leads, Customers, Bookings, Jobs, Invoices. Blue = a page name that also changes. The bottom strip shows how each set fits the phone bottom bar (red = too long for 5 tabs). Local page only (D19).

Why change the current names

  • Overview holds a page called Today: two names for one place.
  • Work is empty in Lite mode (Bookings and Jobs are extras, D63), and means little to a salon.
  • Money and Setup are fine but read like an accounting app; owners say “payments” and “my business”.
  • Owners think in the flow: Lead → Booking / Job → Invoice → Paid. Names that follow it are easier to find.
